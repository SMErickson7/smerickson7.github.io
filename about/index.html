About me

I like to know how things work before I explain why they matter.

I'm Steven, a B2B marketer based in Plainville, Massachusetts. This site is where I keep the writing and side projects that come out of that habit.

How I work

My approach is direct. Before I can generate demand for something, I need a thorough, ground-level understanding of the product, the customer, and exactly why they choose it. Once I have that foundation, the ideas for how to reach them come flooding in.

That same instinct drives my side projects. When an app frustrates me, I map out where the friction is and redesign it. When I need a part, I model it and print it. The workshop and the writing on this site are what that curiosity leaves behind.

What I do professionally

For {{ site.time | date: "%Y" | minus: 2012 }} years I've worked at the intersection of marketing, technology, and operations, bridging the gap between "we built it" and "they bought it." That has meant bringing complex new products to market, launching B2B e-commerce platforms, enabling channel partners, and building the automation and data connections that let marketing and sales work from the same picture of the customer.

Most recently I was Digital Enablement Manager at Lenze Americas. Before that, I worked in marketing at LendingClub and Dentsply. The full details live in my resume.
