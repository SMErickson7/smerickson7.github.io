About Me

For over years, I've worked at the intersection of marketing, technology, and operations. My approach to B2B marketing is direct. Before I can generate demand, I need a thorough, ground-level understanding of the product, the customer, and exactly why they choose that solution. Once I have that foundation, the ideas for how to reach them come flooding in.

Bridging the gap between "we built it" and "they bought it."

I'm a Senior B2B Marketing Professional who focuses on removing friction from the buying journey. For me, that means proactively getting the most valuable information in front of the customer before they even have to ask, while continually reaffirming their decision by strongly promoting the brand. But great messaging is only half the equation. I rely heavily on analytics, CRM architecture, and system data to determine exactly when and how to communicate with that audience.

I've spent a decade bringing complex new products to market, launching B2B e-commerce platforms, and enabling channel partners. Whether I'm crafting a value proposition for a major product launch or engineering a custom workflow automation to seamlessly nurture prospects, I use data-driven insights to validate strategies and accelerate lead generation.

Today, I collaborate with product and sales teams to translate complex technical requirements into clear messaging that actively builds the brand. My core focus is always on creating, executing, and analyzing strategic marketing campaigns that drive measurable growth. When I step away from my desk, I apply that same passion for continuous improvement to my personal interests. You can usually find me logging miles on my bike, designing custom projects on my 3D printer, or volunteering my time to help the Boylston Schul-Verein put on festivals and run their adult language school.
