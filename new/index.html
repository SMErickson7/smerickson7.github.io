About me

My approach to everything is the same: before I can explain why something matters, I need a ground-level understanding of how it works and why people choose it. Once I have that, the ideas come flooding in.

Professionally, that's meant {{ site.time | date: "%Y" | minus: 2012 }} years in B2B marketing, bridging the gap between "we built it" and "they bought it," most recently as Digital Enablement Manager at Lenze Americas.

Away from the desk, you'll find me logging miles on the bike, designing parts for the 3D printer, or helping the Boylston Schul-Verein run its festivals and adult language school.
